Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does interior design cost in Lucknow?
Interior design in Lucknow typically costs ₹500–₹2,500 per sq ft depending on materials and scope. A standard 3BHK flat (1,200 sq ft) ranges from ₹6 lakh to ₹30 lakh including materials and execution. We offer a free consultation to give you an accurate estimate for your specific project.
Does A Square Homes design duplex houses in Lucknow?
Yes. We have extensive experience designing vastu-compliant duplex homes in Lucknow — both architecture and full interior design. We serve Gomti Nagar, Indira Nagar, Hazratganj, Aliganj and all major areas of Lucknow and surrounding UP.
Do you provide turnkey interior and construction projects?
Yes. We provide complete end-to-end turnkey design and execution in Lucknow — from concept and 3D visualisation to material procurement, vendor management, and final handover. Zero stress for the client.
Which areas of Lucknow do you serve?
A Square Homes serves all major areas of Lucknow including Gomti Nagar, Hazratganj, Indira Nagar, Aliganj, Mahanagar, Vikas Nagar, and surrounding areas of Uttar Pradesh.
Do you offer a free design consultation?
Yes. We offer a free 60-minute design consultation for all new projects. Call us at +91 94548 11455, WhatsApp us, or fill out the contact form and we'll get back within 24 hours.
How long does an interior design project take?
Most residential interior projects in Lucknow take 8–16 weeks from design approval to handover. Full turnkey projects including construction may take 4–6 months depending on the scope and size.
